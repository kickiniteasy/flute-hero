Allow the microphone

Use Safari. Tap this, then approve it in Settings. The approve button is not on this page.

Download the profile

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. At the very top, under your name, tap Profile Downloaded.
  3. If that row is missing, go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and tap Flute Hero Local.
  4. Tap Install, then enter your passcode and tap Install again.
  5. Go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and turn on Flute Hero Local.
  6. Open the game and tap Use the flute.

The Profile Downloaded row disappears after a few minutes. If it is gone, tap the download link again and open Settings right away.
